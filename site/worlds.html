Two hundred real worlds

ORRERY ships five invented rulesets. This is the route from there to a catalogue of actual planets and moons — tidally locked worlds, moons with oceans under ice, planets orbiting brown dwarfs, pulsars and white dwarfs, and planets orbiting nothing at all — built so that each world is a consequence of its parameters rather than a hand-painted skin. Engine and product items here are a roadmap; the in-app Worlds picker only lists playable bodies.
