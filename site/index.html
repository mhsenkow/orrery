What you can do

Spin a world. Raise crust. Seed life. Start a fire and watch smoke and animals move. Descend into a patch of the map and read what your hand actually changed.

  • Vandal — one spectacular act in seconds (Strike tools).
  • Gardener — leave it running; come back to a changed place.
  • Tinkerer — coupled Earth-system toys with published limits.

Why it exists

SimEarth proved planetary play; almost nobody advanced the embodied half — being large, then small, in one continuous world. ORRERY bets that scale is the genre’s missing verb.
