Where the prototype actually is

  • The clock. 200 years a tick, uniform. Eras are named every 48,000 years from an eight-branch if chain. There is no geologic timescale, and the Hadean is 24 silent warmup ticks with the biosphere paused so the globe does not turn green.
  • Life. One density array and one class index, against eight hard-coded classes from prokaryote to mammal. Advancement is gated on a year counter, not a condition. Trophic structure is three global scalars. No genome, no lineage, no tree.
  • Chemistry. Seven gases, a log-forcing greenhouse, a one-line weathering term, and photosynthesis as two magic constants. Oxygen accumulates directly from photosynthesis rather than from carbon burial — the wrong mechanism, in a way that matters.
  • The good parts. Plate tectonics with classified boundaries, hotspots, stream-power erosion, a closed water budget, three-cell circulation, obliquity, sea ice separated from land ice, and a 120-world catalogue. The geosphere is well ahead of the biosphere, and that gap is the opportunity.
  • The picture. WebGL2, a cube-sphere with vertex colours from per-ruleset colour functions, an analytic rim, a cloud shell, and sixteen SVG sprites on billboards. Well made, and a diagram of a planet rather than a planet.
