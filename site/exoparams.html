Where the catalogue actually is

    On the figures. Every value quoted is the current consensus where consensus exists, and flagged as contested where it does not. The seed parameter table lives in scripts/exoparams.mjs and was assembled by hand — it is a starting draft, and item “Cross-check the seed table” exists because it certainly contains errors. Check against the NASA Exoplanet Archive pscomppars table before treating anything here as authoritative.
